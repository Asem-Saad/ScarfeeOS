# Contributing

ScarfeeOS is created and led by Asem Y Saad. Contributions and revisions from the open source community are welcome.

## Sign your commits
Every commit needs a Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) sign-off, which certifies that you have the right to submit the work. See https://developercertificate.org.

```
git commit -s
```

This adds a line with your own name and email:

```
Signed-off-by: Your Name <your.email@example.com>
```

## Your rights
Contributions are licensed under Apache-2.0 (see `LICENSE`). You keep the copyright to your work, and you may add your own copyright statement to your changes.

## Before you submit
- Follow the [IP and licensing policy](docs/ip-and-licensing.md).
- Every user-facing change must work in English and Arabic, including right-to-left layout. See [docs/i18n-and-arabic.md](docs/i18n-and-arabic.md).
- Run the tests: `cargo test` in `core/`.
